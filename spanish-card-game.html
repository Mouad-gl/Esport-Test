How to Play:
  • Match the suit or number of the top card.
  • 1 (Ace) — Skips the next player's turn.
  • 2 — Next player must draw 2 cards (stackable!).
  • 7 — You choose the new active suit.
  • If you can't play, click the deck to draw.
  • First player to empty their hand wins!
